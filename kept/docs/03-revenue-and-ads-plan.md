# Revenue model & ads plan

Kept makes money in two ways that never conflict with the member:
- a consumer subscription;
- an employer/health-plan fee per covered life.

There are no ads in the product, no data sales and no drug margin.

The "ads plan" below is how *we* buy attention to grow, not how we sell the attention of our members.

## 1. Pricing

| Tier | Price | Who | What |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | $0 | Anyone on a GLP-1 | Dose log, calendar reminders, side-effect coach with red flags, weight trend, protein/fiber/water targets, daily check-in, data export |
| **Kept Plus** | $9.99/month or $69/year | Members paying themselves | Three-day strength program (gym or home), muscle-protection score, off-ramp planner and regain guardrail, cost finder, appeal letter, doctor-ready report |
| **Kept for Work** | $0.85 per covered life per month (PMPM) | Employers, health plans, TPAs | Kept Plus for every member via enrollment code, de-identified dashboard, persistence reporting, condition-of-coverage program documentation |

Why these numbers:
- **$9.99/month** sits under the psychological $10 line and below meal-kit or coaching apps.
- **$69/year** is a 42% discount that pulls cash forward and matches the 6–12-month treatment horizon.
- **$0.85 PMPM on covered lives (not users)** gives the benefits team a fixed line item. For a 4,200-life employer that is $42,840 a year — about 4% of the monthly drug spend on ~85 members at $1,050.
- **Volume tiers:** $0.70 at ≥ 25k lives, $0.55 at ≥ 100k lives, with health-plan pricing negotiated.

**Path to $100M ARR**
- **Employers and plans:** ~9M covered lives at an average of ~$0.80 PMPM ≈ $86M. That is ~5% of ~165M people with employer-sponsored coverage.
- **Consumer:** ~180k paying members at $7.20/month ≈ $16M, from ~3M free members — under 10% of US adults on a GLP-1.

## 3. Why employers buy now

1. **They are cutting or tightening coverage, and tightening needs a program.**
   - Business Group on Health's 2026 survey shows weight-management coverage falling from 72% to 60%.
   - 14% have dropped or plan to drop coverage by 2027.
   - Common alternatives to dropping: higher BMI thresholds, biometric checks, and mandatory participation in a weight-management program.
   - Kept is the lowest-friction program a member can actually complete.
2. **They can't see persistence.** Kept's dashboard leads with it, against the ~35% benchmark, plus "drug spend kept productive".
3. **Drug spend is the problem, not program spend.** At ~$1,050 per member per month, each extra member still on therapy at month 12 represents $12,600 of drug spend that produced a durable result instead of being followed by regain. For a 4,200-life employer with ~85 members on therapy, about four extra members retained matches the annual Kept fee of $42,840.

**Channels**
- Benefits consultants: Mercer, WTW, Segal, Lockton and regional brokers.
- TPAs and stop-loss carriers.
- Point solutions that already sell to the same buyer: EAP, MSK.
- Direct sales to self-insured employers with 1–20k lives.
- Lilly Employer Connect and similar manufacturer-direct employer programs, where Kept is the neutral support layer.

## 4. Ads plan (paid and owned acquisition)

**Principles**
- No before/after body imagery and no "lose X lbs" claims.
- No pricing that implies we sell the drug.
- Every ad says what Kept is ("a companion, not a prescriber").
- Health-related ad policies (Meta, TikTok, Google) are met by targeting interests and content, never inferred health conditions.

**Channel 1 — Creators (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts): ~45% of the paid budget**
- 30–50 micro-creators already documenting a GLP-1 journey (10k–250k followers), on paid-per-post plus an affiliate code for Plus.
- Formats:
  - "What I eat on shot day for 100 g protein"
  - "My day-2 nausea fix"
  - "I'm tapering off — here's my plan"
  - "My employer dropped coverage, here's what I'm paying now"
- Target: $2.50 CAC per free sign-up, measured by code and landing UTM.

**Channel 2 — Search (Google, Bing): ~25%**
- High-intent, no-brand keywords: "zepbound missed dose", "wegovy nausea day 2", "how much protein on ozempic", "coming off wegovy", "zepbound cost without insurance", "foundayo vs zepbound".
- Each keyword maps to an owned page (see SEO).
- Target: $4 CAC per free sign-up.

**Channel 3 — Reddit and communities: ~10%**
- Promoted posts in r/Zepbound, r/Semaglutide and r/loseit with useful tools (missed-dose calculator, protein-target calculator) that end in the waitlist.
- Founders answer questions openly as Kept. No astroturfing.

**Channel 4 — Podcasts and newsletters: ~10%**
- Health and benefits newsletters for the employer funnel: HR Brew, Employee Benefit News, Fierce Healthcare sponsorships.
- Consumer health podcasts for members.

**Channel 5 — Retargeting: ~10%**
- Site visitors only, first-party audiences, no health-condition targeting. Frequency capped.

**Owned and earned (the long-term engine)**
- **SEO pages generated from the same data the app uses:**
  - label-accurate missed-dose pages per drug;
  - side-effect pages per symptom with red flags;
  - protein-target and cost calculators;
  - "Stopping X: the off-ramp" guides.
  Each is reviewed by the clinical advisory board.
- **Referral.** The waitlist moves a member up five places per referral (built). After launch, give a month of Plus and get a month.
- **Pharmacy and prescriber referrals.** A free one-page report and "Kept code" handouts for independent pharmacies and obesity-medicine practices. No payments for referrals of patients.
- **Annual Persistence Report.** Publish de-identified outcomes (persistence, lean-share, regain after taper vs. stop). It earns press and becomes the employer sales deck.

## 5. What we will never sell

- Ads inside the product, or sponsored foods and supplements.
- Member data, de-identified or not, to drugmakers, PBMs, insurers or data brokers.
- Referral fees from pharmacies, telehealth companies or compounders.
- Individual-level data to employers.

These are written into the privacy policy (`/privacy`) and the employer contract.

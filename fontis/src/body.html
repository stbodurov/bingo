How a proof works

From request to decision in four steps

Request

Send a proof link from your leasing or loan system, or by API, with the rule attached, like "monthly income at least 3× rent."

Prove

The applicant signs in to the site that holds their income, in their own session on their phone. Fontis never sees their password.

Decide

You get a proof report: pass or fail against your rule, the source, the date range and a proof ID anyone can re-check.

Reuse

The applicant keeps the proof in a free Fontis wallet and can share it with the next landlord or lender while it's fresh.

AI + cryptography

One makes it work everywhere. The other makes it true.

Integrating source by source is why payroll APIs miss so many workers. Trusting AI to read a screenshot is how forgeries get through. Fontis splits the job.

What the AI does

  • Finds the right pages on any siteEarnings, hours, balances and tenure, across gig apps, payroll portals, banks and marketplaces, with no integration to build first.
  • Adds new sources by itselfWhen an applicant uses a site we haven't seen, the AI writes the extraction for it, so coverage keeps growing.
  • Applies your rules3× rent, 12-month averages, 80 hours, multiple income streams combined.
  • Helps the applicantPlain-language guidance, and a fallback review when no online source exists.

What the cryptography does

  • Proves where the data came fromzkTLS attests that the numbers came from the real site over a genuine encrypted session, and weren't edited afterward.
  • Shares facts, not statementsSelective disclosure reveals only what your rule needs and keeps the rest private.
  • Lets anyone check itEach proof has an ID anyone can verify, with an optional on-chain attestation for audit trails and revocation.
  • Belongs to the applicantProofs live in the applicant's wallet and can be reused across verifiers, so nobody has to prove the same thing twice.

Privacy

Questions

What verifiers ask first

How is a proof different from a screenshot or a PDF?

A screenshot shows what someone claims a site said. A Fontis proof uses zkTLS to attest that the figures came from the real site over a genuine encrypted session and weren't changed afterward. Editing a single digit breaks the proof.

Do applicants need crypto or a wallet app?

No. Applicants open a link, sign in to the site they already use and approve what gets shared. The proof wallet is built in and free. Nothing to buy, and no token.

What if a site isn't supported yet?

The AI can read new sites and write the extraction for them, so coverage grows with use. If there's no online source at all, the applicant can fall back to a reviewed upload, flagged as such in your report.

How is this different from payroll APIs?

Payroll APIs and databases work well for W-2 employees at covered employers. Fontis is built for everyone else, and can sit right after your current provider as the step that catches the applicants it misses.

Is the applicant's data safe?

Applicants sign in on their own device and Fontis never stores passwords. You receive only the facts your rule needs. The applicant can see and revoke what they've shared.

Is Fontis available now?

Fontis is in development. Design partners decide which sources and integrations ship first.

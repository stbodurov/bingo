# How to advertise Fontis

## Who you're selling to

There are three buyers, and they're large in number. Each sends thousands of paid proofs:

1. **Property managers and leasing teams.** Start with Sun Belt multifamily, where rental fraud is highest. Titles: VP Operations, Leasing Director, Head of Resident Screening, Fraud/Risk.
2. **Consumer, auto and BNPL lenders and fintechs** with gig-heavy applicant pools. Titles: Head of Credit, Underwriting, Fraud, Product Lead for Onboarding.
3. **Medicaid managed-care plans** facing the Dec 31, 2026 work requirements. Titles: VP Member Services, Eligibility and Enrollment, Medicaid Operations.

Applicants are the fourth group. They don't pay, but every proof request introduces Fontis to one of them, and their reusable proofs pull in the next verifier.

**Goal for the first 90 days:** 10 design-partner pilots and 3 paid contracts. These are targets, not forecasts.

**Message rules:**

- Lead with **fraud and the applicants you can't verify**, not with "zkTLS" or "crypto."
- The cryptography is the reason to trust the result. Mention it in one line, with a link to the explanation.

## Channels, in order of expected return

### 1. Founder-led outbound (main channel, ~$150/month in tools)

- **Build a list of about 400 accounts:**
  - The 150 largest US property managers (NMHC 50 and similar lists).
  - About 150 fintech and auto lenders.
  - About 100 Medicaid MCOs and plans.
- **Find 2–3 contacts per account** with LinkedIn Sales Navigator, then Apollo or Clay for work emails.
- **Send a three-touch sequence over 10 days**, with the free 500-application pilot as the offer. Example:

  > Subject: the renters your screening can't verify
  >
  > 12% of rental applications now contain fraud, mostly forged income documents, and gig and self-employed renters aren't in payroll databases at all.
  >
  > Fontis lets applicants prove income straight from the app or portal that pays them: a proof that can't be edited, checked against your 3× rule in about two minutes.
  >
  > We're running free pilots on 500 applications as the step after your current screening. Worth a look? [link?utm_source=email&utm_medium=outbound&utm_campaign=leasing]

- **Stay compliant.** Include a physical address and an opt-out in every email (CAN-SPAM). Honor opt-outs immediately.

### 2. LinkedIn: founder posts and targeted ads ($2,000–$4,000/month)

- **Posts, 2–3 a week:**
  - "Here's a fake pay stub made in 30 seconds. Here's why a proof can't be faked."
  - Breakdowns of public fraud data.
  - How Medicaid hours verification will work for gig workers.
- **Ads:**
  - **Targeting:** job titles above, in Real Estate/Property Management, Financial Services/Lending and Health Insurance; plus a company-list upload of your 400 accounts.
  - **Formats:** Document Ads (a "Rental fraud in the AI era" playbook) and Lead Gen Forms.
  - **Expect** roughly $6–15 per click. Judge channels by qualified pilots, not clicks.
- **Ad copy to test:**
  - "Your applicants can fake a pay stub in seconds. They can't fake a Fontis proof."
  - "1 in 3 applicants isn't in payroll databases. Verify them anyway: from the source, in two minutes."
  - "Medicaid work requirements start Dec 31. Can your gig-worker members prove their hours?"

### 3. Lead magnets that sell ($0, about a week of work)

- **"Spot the fake" quiz.** Ten pay stubs and bank statements, half AI-generated. Most people score badly, which makes the point. It's shareable on LinkedIn.
- **Fraud cost calculator.** Units × fraud rate × average loss per bad tenancy gives a dollar number to bring to a pilot call.
- **The Rental Fraud Playbook (PDF).** Gated by the waitlist form.

### 4. Industry events (travel and meetings first; booths later)

- **Leasing:** NAA Apartmentalize, NMHC events, regional apartment associations.
- **Lending:** Money20/20 and regional lending conferences.
- **Health plans:** AHIP and Medicaid health plan association meetings.

Book meetings from the outbound list before you go. Host one small breakfast for leasing or fraud leaders.

### 5. Partnerships and integrations (the long-term engine)

- **Marketplaces:** list on property-management and leasing-software marketplaces, and on loan-origination system marketplaces. Integrations put Fontis inside the existing verification flow.
- **Screening partners:** partner with tenant-screening and background-check resellers who need a gig-income answer.

### 6. Earned media and community ($0)

- **Press:** pitch housing, lending and health-policy reporters with data from pilots. For example, "X% of unverifiable renters were real, and Y% of uploaded stubs failed."
- **Crypto and zk community:** a technical post on the zkTLS design. This builds credibility with investors and hires, not buyers.

### 7. Google Search ($500–1,000/month)

- Keywords: rental application fraud, fake pay stub detection, income verification for gig workers, self-employed income verification, Medicaid work requirement verification.
- Low volume, but these searchers are in the market now.

## What not to spend on

- Consumer ads to applicants. They arrive through verifiers for free.
- Crypto media and X ads, except for the technical credibility post.
- Booths before you have pilot results to show.

## First 30 days

| Week | Do |
|---|---|
| 1 | Domain, deploy the page, privacy policy, analytics. Build the 400-account list. Start the "spot the fake" quiz |
| 2 | Outbound at 30 contacts a day. Two founder posts. LinkedIn ads at $75/day, two variants |
| 3 | Publish the quiz and playbook; retarget site visitors. Book event meetings |
| 4 | Kill the weaker ad. Start the first pilots. Turn pilot numbers into the next post |

**Budget for the first 90 days:** about $3,000–5,000 a month, mostly LinkedIn. Outbound, lead magnets and partnerships cost time, not money.

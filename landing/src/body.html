Platform

Four parts, one record per asset

AI

Examiners

AI agents that do a field examiner's work on every asset, every day. They read the tape and the documents, then check them against title records, VINs, bank receipts, shipment data and the debtor's real domain.

Onchain

Registry

A public ledger of asset fingerprints. When a second lender registers the same asset, both are alerted at once. No borrower names, amounts or deal terms are ever written to it.

Onchain

Liens

New loans can be issued as lien tokens, which are controllable electronic records under UCC Article 12. The lender holding the token has priority over any filing, and the asset moves into a securitization by transfer instead of re-papering.

AI Onchain

Oracle

Signed, per-asset attestations from the examiners, published onchain. Tokenization platforms and lending protocols can check that a token is backed by a verified, unencumbered asset before accepting it.

AI + chain

Each does the part the other can't

AI is good at judging messy evidence and bad at being a neutral record. A blockchain is a neutral record and can't see the real world. Clearlien needs both.

What the AI does Examiners

  • Reads what borrowers sendLoan tapes, receivables agings, invoices, statements, in any format.
  • Checks it against the worldTitle and lien records, bank receipts, shipment data, the debtor's authenticated domain.
  • Spots forgery and inflationLook-alike domains, edited statements, a 2014 car funded as a 2022.
  • Decides what's an exceptionAnd attaches the evidence a credit officer needs.

What the chain does Registry, Liens, Oracle

  • Keeps one neutral recordNo lender, borrower or vendor, Clearlien included, can rewrite who registered an asset first.
  • Makes a second pledge visibleEvery lender checks the same ledger, so a collision can't hide in someone else's database.
  • Gives the lender controlA lien token held in the lender's own custody. Under Article 12, control beats any filing.
  • Lets credit move onchainVerified, unencumbered loans can be financed by tokenized funds and lending protocols.

Article 12 took effect in New York on 3 June 2026 and has been enacted in 33 states and DC. Lien tokens are optional; examiners and the registry work for any facility today.

Security

The ledger knows an asset exists. Not whose it is.

Examiners get read-only access to the tape, servicing exports and collections account, which most facility agreements already require. Your data stays in your account and is never written to the ledger.

The ledger holds fingerprints and timestamps. When two lenders register the same asset, each is told there's a collision. Who the other lender is stays private until both choose to talk. Lien tokens sit in your own custody, not ours.

Questions

What people ask first

Why does this need a blockchain?

A double pledge works because each lender only sees its own records. A shared registry fixes that only if no single company can edit it or quietly favor a customer, and that includes us. A public ledger gives every lender the same record of who registered an asset first. It's also what lets a lien token give a lender control under UCC Article 12.

Why does this need AI?

A ledger only records what it's told. If the asset doesn't exist, or the invoice was generated in ten minutes, an onchain record of it is worthless. The examiners check each asset against independent sources before anything is registered, across thousands of assets a day.

Do we have to hold crypto?

No. There's no token to buy or trade. Examiners and registry alerts work like any other software. Lien tokens are optional and can sit with your existing digital-asset custodian.

What does the borrower have to share?

Read-only access to the loan tape or receivables aging, servicing exports and the collections account. Most facility agreements already require this for reporting and field exams.

How is it priced?

Lenders pay on the collateral monitored, in basis points per year, with a minimum per facility. Originators pay a small fee per registered asset. Design partners get their first facility checked at no cost.

Is Clearlien available now?

Clearlien is in development. Early-access lenders and platforms decide which asset classes and integrations ship first.

Questions

The fine print, up front.

Is Harbormaster a customs broker or a law firm?

Neither. Harbormaster is software that computes, documents and drafts. Your licensed customs broker files CAPE declarations, corrections and protests; court actions go through trade counsel. We work with yours or introduce you to partners.

Who actually receives the refund?

CBP refunds the importer of record. If your supplier or forwarder imported on a DDP basis, the refund goes to them — Harbormaster quantifies what was passed through to you in your invoices and drafts the request.

My entries have already liquidated. Is it too late?

Not necessarily. Within 180 days of liquidation, a protest keeps the entry open. Past that, CAPE Phase 3 currently requires CIT plaintiff status; the court certified an importer class on Oct 8, 2026, and §1581(i) actions have a two-year limit from the payment. We show which bucket each entry is in.

How accurate is the tariff engine?

Every layer is computed from the measure's effective dates, exemptions and stacking rules, and shows the authority it relies on. Rules are versioned and re-tested against CBP CSMS guidance as it changes; anything we're unsure of is flagged for review rather than guessed.

What data do you need, and how is it protected?

Entry summary line data (ES-003 or 7501 exports) and, for margin, your SKU costs and prices. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, isolated per workspace, never used to train models, and deletable on request.

Where does AI fit?

Language, not numbers. Claude reads product descriptions for classification and answers questions by calling the engine. Duties, deadlines and refund routes are deterministic and auditable.

# Sundial — revenue model & ads plan

## Revenue
| Line | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Plan | Free | Full engine, save plans. The acquisition channel. |
| Paycheck | $19/mo or $190/yr | Automated monthly paycheck, tax/IRMAA withholding, RMDs and conversions on schedule, guardrail alerts, 2 planner sessions/yr |
| Managed | 0.30%/yr of assets, capped at $3,000 | RIA-managed portfolio behind the paycheck; dedicated fee-only CFP® |
| Annuity distribution | Carrier commission, ~1–2.5% of premium, disclosed and capped at 2% net | SPIA, DIA/QLAC, MYGA only — no high-surrender products |
| B2B engine | $2–6 per participant per year | Recordkeepers and employers offering in-plan decumulation; white-label claiming + paycheck |

Guardrails: no data sales, no ads inside the product, no commission visibility in the engine.

## Funnel targets (year 1)
Visitor → free plan 12% · plan → account 35% · account → Paycheck 8% · Paycheck → Managed
15% · plans with an essentials gap → annuity quote request 9%.

## Ads plan (year 1 budget $1.6M)
The audience is 55–75, plus their adult children (35–55) who often drive the decision.

| Channel | Tactic | Budget | KPI |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Search | "when should I take social security", "social security spousal benefit", "roth conversion before RMD", "how much can I spend in retirement", "annuity rates" → quick claiming calculator / free plan | $420k | Cost per completed plan < $28 |
| YouTube & CTV | 30-second "your first retirement paycheck" spots on finance/news channels; retargeting plan starters | $380k | Cost per plan < $45; brand lift 55+ |
| Facebook | 55+ lookalikes of plan completers; creative = claiming-age result cards ("Claim at 70 — +$95k") | $300k | CPA < $35 |
| Podcasts & newsletters | Host-read on retirement/personal-finance shows (e.g. retirement planning podcasts, AARP-adjacent newsletters) | $180k | Promo-code plans; payback < 9 months |
| Adult-children campaign | "Help your parents plan" share links; Instagram/TikTok creators 30–45 | $120k | 25% of plans via share links |
| Partnerships | Credit unions, employers' HR benefits, CPAs in tax season (referral fee) | $120k | 40 partners |
| PR & SEO | Quarterly "Retirement Paycheck Index" from anonymised plans; COLA/Trustees-report explainers | $80k | 80k organic visits/mo by month 12 |

### Seasonality
- **October** — COLA announcement and Medicare open enrolment (IRMAA content).
- **January–April** — tax season (Roth conversions, RMDs, senior deduction).
- **Trustees Report release** — Social Security shortfall explainers.
- **Birthdays** — 61½–62 and 64½–65 triggered email nurture for account holders.

### Waitlist mechanics (live)
Early access to the paycheck service is ordered by signup time, and each referral moves a
person ten places ahead; plan summaries attached to signups let us prioritise people
retiring soonest.
